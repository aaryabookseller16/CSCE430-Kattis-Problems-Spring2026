# Upsolve Topics

This folder groups extra Lab 2 problems solved after the main lab. The main themes are online median maintenance and grid reachability.

## Problems
- **Cookie Selection:** uses two heaps so the upper median is available quickly. Example: with cookies 1,2,3,4, the upper median is 3. Complexity: O(log n) per operation.
- **Railway Runner:** uses BFS over a 3-column grid with special rules for rails, trains, and ladders. Example: a ladder below a rail cell forces movement to the train roof two rows down. Complexity: O(N).
