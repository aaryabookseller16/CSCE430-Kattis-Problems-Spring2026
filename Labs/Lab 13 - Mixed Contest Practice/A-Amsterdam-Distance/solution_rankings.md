# Solution Rankings

1. `solution_formula.py`
   - Method: compare going through the center with going around at the smaller ring
   - Time: `O(1)`
   - Fastest version

2. `solution.py`
   - Method: try every possible meeting ring from `0` to `N`
   - Time: `O(N)`
   - Still easily fast enough because `N <= 100`, and it is simpler to trust
